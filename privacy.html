Nistikon — Orthodox Fasting Calendar & Prayer Rule
Effective August 15, 2026
Our Commitment: The developer of Nistikon collects no personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or developer-operated servers. Bundled calendar and prayer content works offline on your device.

Information We Collect

Nistikon does not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts. The fasting calendar, saints, and prayers are bundled into the app and read directly from your device. Your device may communicate with the App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android when you load, purchase, or restore subscriptions. Legal, support, and store links that you choose to open leave the app and are handled by Apple, Google, or your browser.

  • Prayer Rule & Settings: Your prayer-rule selections, jurisdiction, appearance, and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • No usage data: We do not record which screens you view, which prayers you read, or how you fast.

Notifications

Nistikon can send optional local reminders for your prayer rule and for fasting days. These notifications are:

  • Scheduled and delivered entirely on your device
  • Local notifications only — no push server is involved
  • Fully controlled by your device's notification settings, and off by default

Third-Party Services

Nistikon uses no analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or external-content SDKs. On iOS, purchases use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services. On Android, purchases use Google Play Billing and Google Play services. Apple or Google may process the store, account, payment, device, and diagnostic information required to provide those services under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your card or bank details. See the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions

Nistikon offers an optional premium subscription (monthly or yearly). Subscription management and payment processing are handled by the store where you subscribed — the App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android. We never receive your payment-card or bank information.

Children's Privacy

Nistikon is intended for a general audience and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Religious Content

Nistikon is a reference and practice aid. Fasting information is general religious guidance, not medical or dietary advice. Always follow the direction of your own priest or spiritual father.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
